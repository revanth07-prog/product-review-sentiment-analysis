About This Project

Technical specification, architecture, theoretical foundation, and methodology.

1. Problem Statement & Objectives

E-commerce product reviews provide invaluable consumer feedback but are predominantly unstructured, noisy, and imbalanced. The objective is to construct an end-to-end deep learning system that autonomously cleans product reviews, aligns words with semantic vector representations, captures sequential context via an LSTM network, and delivers real-time sentiment predictions through an interactive web interface.

2. Flipkart Reviews Dataset

Sourced from Kaggle containing over 189,000 real Flipkart product reviews. Reviews contain product titles, review headers, detailed descriptions, and numerical star ratings (1 to 5 stars). The pipeline cleans invalid rows, combines review headers with description bodies, removes duplicates, and maps ratings to sentiment labels (1-2★ Negative, 3★ Neutral, 4-5★ Positive).

3. Stanford GloVe Word Embeddings

Global Vectors for Word Representation (GloVe) maps words into a continuous 100-dimensional vector space based on global word-word co-occurrence statistics from Wikipedia and Gigaword. Utilizing pre-trained GloVe vectors grants the model rich semantic understanding and synoymous relationships even before domain training commences.

4. LSTM Neural Network Architecture

Long Short-Term Memory networks overcome the vanishing gradient problem in traditional Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) using input, forget, and output gating mechanisms. This enables the model to retain long-range dependencies, contextual negations (such as "not good at all"), and contrastive clauses across the review text.

5. Methodology & Training Details

Preprocessing: HTML stripping, contraction expansion (e.g. "can't" → "can not"), URL removal, punctuation cleaning, lowercasing, and Keras tokenization with post-padding.
Vocabulary Alignment: The embedding matrix maps vocabulary indices to GloVe vectors. Unseen domain-specific words are assigned initialized vectors while padding tokens are masked at index 0.
Regularization: Dropout layers (rate = 0.3) after both LSTM and Dense feature layers prevent overfitting on frequent vocabulary tokens.
Loss & Optimization: Trained with Adam optimizer and sparse_categorical_crossentropy (or binary_crossentropy) loss with EarlyStopping and ModelCheckpoint callbacks.
Evaluation Protocol: Stratified holdout evaluation reporting Accuracy, Macro Precision, Macro Recall, and Macro F1 to prevent majority-class bias.

6. Known Limitations & Considerations

  • Domain Nuance: GloVe was trained on Wikipedia/Gigaword; heavy Indian colloquialisms or transliterated Hinglish phrases common in Flipkart reviews may fall out-of-vocabulary (OOV).
  • Sarcasm & Mixed Sentiment: Complex reviews containing mixed sentiment (e.g., "Great design but battery dies in 10 minutes") require attention mechanisms or aspect-based sentiment analysis for fine-grained decomposition.
  • Sequence Length Truncation: Reviews exceeding 100 tokens are truncated, which could discard context positioned at the tail end of long reviews.
